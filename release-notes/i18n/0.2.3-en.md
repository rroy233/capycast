# Release v0.2.3

## ✨ New Features

- **Autoplay for collection videos**: When you cast a video that belongs to a Bilibili collection, a prompt appears in the bottom-right corner once playback starts and the playback controls have been dismissed. Confirm "Enable autoplay" with the remote within 5 seconds and the next item continues automatically when the current video ends: for multi-part videos the next part plays first, then the next video in the collection, so you no longer need the phone to keep going. Ignoring the prompt or letting the countdown expire keeps the existing behaviour.

## Usage Notes

- The prompt only appears when the video really is part of a collection, and it is asked at most once per cast.
- Autoplay applies to the current cast only and is not carried over to the next one; casting something else, stopping the cast, a playback error or restarting the app all require enabling it again.
- Once enabled, "The next video will play soon" appears in the last 5 seconds, and each continued video is reported as its own watch-history entry.
- While the prompt is visible, the remote's D-pad centre/Enter key activates the prompt button instead of opening the playback controls.
- Autoplay covers regular user-submitted videos; anime, film and other PGC content and live streams keep their existing behaviour.
