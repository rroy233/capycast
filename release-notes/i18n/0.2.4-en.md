# Release v0.2.4

## Highlights

This release makes casting follow what your TV can actually decode: VOD playback now prefers the codec your TV supports in hardware and switches codec automatically when frames keep dropping. Danmaku is back in sync with the picture, and the app no longer keeps playing audio once it leaves the foreground.

## ✨ New Features

- **Codec chosen for your TV's decoder**: When one quality offers several codecs (H.264 / HEVC / AV1), the app now prefers the one your TV can decode in hardware, so a stream is less likely to have a line that cannot actually play. The codec shown in the quality list is the one actually used, and AV1 streams now appear in the quality options.
- **Automatic codec switch when playback stutters**: If frames keep dropping, the app switches to another decodable codec at the same quality and continues from the current position without changing the quality level. When no alternative codec is left for that quality, it shows "This quality is not playing smoothly and no other codec is available".

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Danmaku stays in sync with the picture**: Fixed danmaku that could lag behind or drift out of alignment with the video, for example after a short picture stall or after changing playback speed. Danmaku is now scheduled on the video timeline, so pause, seek and speed changes stay consistent.
- **No more audio in the background**: Pressing Home, switching to another app or turning the TV screen off now pauses playback and releases the player instead of continuing audio and decoder resources in the background. Coming back restores the previous position, playback speed and volume.

## Usage Notes

- After returning to the app, playback is paused: press play once to continue. The video is reopened in a paused state, so a short buffering moment may follow.
- While the app is in the background, casting and remote commands from your phone do not take effect; return to the app and try again.
